OVERVIEW

The Hollowed are described in multiple independent accounts as tall, featureless humanoids composed of a viscous black substance (commonly likened to ink or ichor). Observers report small, circular white or red eyes. The phenomenon is notable for two primary threats: direct aggression (physical attacks) and an identity-replacement infection that converts victims into new Hollowed forms.

FIELD NOTE (excerpt)

"It stood in the doorway. No face. Two pinpricks of light — one red — like lenses. When it touched Jeremy he didn't die. He just... stopped being Jeremy." — Anonymous, 2011

NOTABLE CASES

  • Redwater, TX — 2005: Security cam captured a dark humanoid entering an apartment before the occupant reappeared without memory.
  • Cedar Ridge High — 2018: Multiple students reported a peer covered in black streaks who later exhibited flat affect and repetitive speech.
  • I-70 Rest Stop — 2021: Truck driver reported a figure leaving black prints on asphalt; nearby dashcam corrupted for 2 minutes.

INFECTION / "HOLLOWING"

Community-compiled pattern:

  1. Observation — repeated glimpses near targeted individuals.
  2. Residue — black streaks or drips noticed on clothing or walls.
  3. Behavioral shift — monotone speech, forgetfulness, loss of motivation.
  4. Conversion or Collapse — subject either collapses (attack) or reawakens functioning as an infected agent.

WARNING: Reports indicate prolonged exposure increases risk of takeover. Remain skeptical; none of these incidents are officially verified.
